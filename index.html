How it's governed

Governed by three rules.

01

Harness over model

The model is replaceable. The harness — permissions, guardrails, the audit trail — is what we build, and what you're actually buying.

02

Least privilege

Every agent gets the smallest set of permissions it needs for the task in front of it. Nothing standing, nothing broad.

03

Human-in-the-loop, tiered by reversibility and blast radius

How much say you get scales with how hard an action is to undo, and how far its consequences reach.

Human oversight is the architecture. Not a feature.

Review scales with reversibility: low-stakes actions run, higher-stakes actions surface for review, irreversible actions wait for your confirmation. Agents run in isolated VMs, credentials encrypted, actions logged with timestamps. Regulated data — clinical notes, financial records, PHI — is flagged before processing. Architected for SOC 2, HIPAA, and NERC CIP compliance paths.

The loop

Deploy. Work. Review. Compound.

01

Deploy

We set up an AI agent for your operation. It inherits corrections from prior deployments — hundreds of behavioral refinements on day one.

02

Work

Your agent handles research, email, scheduling, vendor comparison, financial analysis, document generation. Whatever the operation needs — and not only during business hours.

03

Review

Higher-stakes actions surface for human review before they go out. When something's wrong, the correction is captured and carried forward, so the agent stops repeating it.

04

Compound

A fix in one deployment can improve others in the network — not just yours. The correction library grows over time, and the improvements compound across the fleet.

Most AI tools reset every conversation. FleetBrain agents accumulate. Corrections persist, and compound across the fleet.
